## Required evidence per candidate

- Exact commit tested.
- Licence and runtime requirements.
- Install/test commands and real outputs.
- Whether credentials are required for each mode.
- Public-data or fixture smoke result.
- Security and custody findings.
- Profitability claims classified as reproduced, unsupported, or not applicable.
- Verdict: validated, partial, or invalidated for our use case.
